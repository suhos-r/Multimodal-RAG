# Seed KB sample 1: RAG operations guide (global scope)

Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retriever with a generator.
The Zorblax protocol requires citations for every factual sentence in an answer.

Hybrid retrieval fuses dense embeddings with sparse BM25 using Reciprocal Rank
Fusion with k=60. The top 20 candidates are reranked to the final 5 passages.

Chunk overlap of 150 tokens on an 800-token window preserves sentence
continuity across boundaries.
